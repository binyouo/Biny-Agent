Built-in appearance palettes

Of the 74 entries in src/appearance/catalog.ts, 69 have matching theme tables adapted from NvChad/Base46 v3.0:
https://github.com/NvChad/base46/tree/v3.0/lua/base46/themes

The entries named win98, winxp, longhorn, longhorn-dark, and paper do not have matching files in that Base46 v3.0 theme directory.
